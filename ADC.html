The Main Carry of the Game, ADC


The bottom of the map is a little different than the other parts. Down in bot lane there are two roles, the first I will be talking about here is the ADC role. The name ADC stands for Attack Damage Carry, which as the name suggests it is a position which plays the carry role of fights and does damage through primarily the attack damage stat, which is a bit out dated due to mages now being good in the role and not using attack damage. The ADC is part of a duo lane with the Support, who's job is primarily to help you out. The ADC is probably the most straight forward is strict in rules role out of every role in the game. Your job as the ADC is to gain the most value from minions as possible, and survive until late stages of the game. Early on ADCs do not have much agency to go off and make their own decisions, they are frequently tied very heavily to their main resource of minions and play more to build leads either by fighting the 2 verses 2 lane of very slowly through gold from minions. The reason the duo lane is in the bottom instead of the top of the map is because the very important objective dragon spawns on the bottom part of the map, and its better to have more teammates near the objective when it spawns. ADC being the role with the least agency also means it has the least variety of characters in the lane, so I will break down the types of primarily marksman characters that are in the role.


Marksman

Marksman champions are long ranges characters that deal damage primarily though basic attacks. They are normally very weak early on in the game, but given enough time to get stronger they slowly become the main source of damage for the team. There are two main types of marksman I will cover, which will be the early game and late game marksman. Early game marksman are champions that want to fight with their support to kill the enemy bot laners instead of playing very slow and reserved. They are more willing to take risks and go for punishing enemy mistakes rather than smooth sailing to victory. While stronger early, they are normally still very strong in later stages of the game, but with unique drawbacks like shorter range or weaknesses built into the champions kit like extra steps to do damage. Late game scaling champions are a bit more of what you would traditionally expect from a scaling champion. They want to play it safe and avoid risk when possible because they will inevitably get very strong by the end of the game if they stick to the safe gameplan.


Mage Bot

Mages are also played in the ADC role. Carry mages are a newer addition to the ADC role, using their long range and ability to shove minion waves easily, they are very safe options that can guarantee a lot more safty then a traditional marksman can. Mages play a similar role in being able to get very strong in the late stages of the game, with the added benefit on normally not needing to rely on supports to help as much because they normally have a bit more self sustainability built into their kits seeing as they're designed for solo lanes. This helps open up the support role to do other things like roam elsewhere on the map and help other allies build leads, which staying very far away and safely being able to play a 1 verses 2 lane.



ADC is a very good role for a very specific kind of player. If you really enjoy the idea of slowly building strength and being the core part of what deals damage for the team, ADC is a great place to look. ADC players need to be good at positioning more than any other role because most players in the game have a goal to specifically hunt you down in fights, one wrong step and you can lose the game. ADC is a very strict role, but also has a lot of general rules and guidelines to follow to be able to replicate the same scenario over and over again, which creates a lot of consistency. If you like the idea of being the carry, biding your time for when its your turn to take control, and making sure you are very aware of everyone trying to get you while you are being protected by your team, then ADC might be the role for you.
