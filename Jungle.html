The Strategist of the Game, Jungle


Jungle is the last (and my favorite) role in the League of legends. Jungle is the most unique role in the entire game, playing a different style of game completely than any other role in the game. Instead of being in a lane with minions to last hit gold from, Junglers have jungle camps in the jungle they fight in order to get gold. There are 6 different jungle camps on each side of the map, and then one and later two more spawn in the river for both Junglers to fight over for a lead. Jungle strays away from the combat of fighting over waves of minions, poking for health advantages, and taking towers to advance the game. Instead, Junglers play based off of what jungle camp you and your opponent have available, but with fog of war meaning you cannot see what the other jungler is doing. If laning is more like playing a fighting game, Jungling would be more like playing chess. Your goals and win conditions are very different depending on what type of champion you play, but the most frequent win condition for junglers in taking objectives like the dragon by bot lane and later in the game baron to end the game. Here are the junglers and their specific ways to deal with jungling:


Full Clear Junglers

Full clearing junglers want to take every camp they have as fast as possible, the faster the camp is taken the faster it will come back, then when all camps are taken from your side of the map you can look around and see if any enemy players are out of position, or if any teammates need help. As a jungler you are not tied to a lane so you can get extra gold by "ganking" lanes and surprise attacking from the jungle. Your primary goal as a full clearing jungler is still to clear as many camps as you possibly can, so if the enemy jungler decides to gank a lane of one of your teammates, and you know their jungle camps are still up, you can go and take their jungle camps that are in a more dangerous position and then return to your safter placed jungle camps and build massive leads from removing their resources while gaining more for yourself. Full clear junglers are carry champions, they want to build as much gold as possible so they can gank whatever target they can find out of position. Many types of champions can be in any sup role of junglers, so full clear junglers you will see tanks, brusiers, assassins, duelists, and even a few mages.


Invading Junglers

Invading junglers are very similar to full clear junglers in every way except one, invading. Invading is when your primary goal, especially early on in the game, is to skip your own camps and in return making the game harder for the enemy jungler by fighting them while theyre trying to take their own jungle camps so you can steal them. Invading is a very high variance playstyle, if your invades are successful you can build massive leads from super early on in the game, but if you lose your invade or the enemy jungler's team comes to help protect him you can also fall massively behind by or losing a lot of time that you could have used building resources.


Ganking Junglers

Some junglers cant take the jungle camps very fast, so full clearing will only slowly put you behind the enemy champion. However, just because you cant take the camps fast, doesn't mean there is nothing to do. Instead on relying on gold and xp from jungle camps, you can play an almost more supportive playstyle by using large gap closing abilities and very fast moving characters to get behind people and make as much chaos early into the game as possible. While this does open up for full clearing junglers to attack your jungle camps, it also creates a massive amount of chaos that many types of champions like mages or other scaling champions dont really want to deal with. This tactic can be used to generate massive leads for specific laners while adding a lot of risk if you fail ganks. Most ganking junglers are pretty tanky and need a bit less gold, which in return means they do a little bit less damage and become a more supportive ally in the later stages of the game.



Jungle is a lot to understand when first trying League of Legends, so I would not recommend jungle to a new player. That being said, after understanding the core of what and how lanes work, I think jungle is an amazing role for people that really like the strategy part of the game. There are endless amounts of strategy to think about in the jungle role, but it being more about strategy also makes it a bit weaker on the side of focusing on mechanical skill. Jungle is a good role for people that like memorizing game states, what win conditions in each game are, and who to play for and how to win the game through a team aspect. Junglers also need very good awareness on what's going on everywhere in the game at all times, and be ready to make game changing calls for the team.
