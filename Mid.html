The Center of the Game, Mid lane


Mid lane is the second solo lane in the game, being the shortest lane in the game due to, well, being in the middle. Mid lane being the shortest lane means minions actually reach mid lane faster than the top or bottom lane. When minions die near you, you get experience points that helps you level up. Every time you level up you get a new point in your of your abilities, which permanently makes that ability stronger. In return, mid lane is primarily focused on characters that either get a lot of value from their abilities, or just really like being able to move around to other places from the center of the map. Mid lane probably has the most diversity out of every lane with playstyles do to having the most options from being in a central position in the game, so ill do my best to give a general overview of what you can expect to see


First ill talk about Mages.

Mages are the main champions that benefit having higher leveled abilities. Typically long ranged, they cast spells to either nuke the minion wave from a far, or poke the enemy champion from a safe range while avoiding taking any fights that could leave themselves vulnerable. There are a lot of different types of mages, but for now ill separate them into two very basic categories; carry and support. Carry mages want to build as many resources as they can by last hitting every minion possible and creating a stable environment for themselves so at the later stages of the game they can defeat other champions with ease. Long range and high ability damage help them because it allows them to interact with others on their terms, minions being the recourse you fight over and being able to kill them very fast means the enemy champion has less options to leave lane and do things elsewhere in the game. Supportive mages are a similar style of play, but they are less prone to avoiding chaos because they don't need the same recourses as a carry mage to be effective. Instead they hope to have another player pull the weight of dealing damage, while they instead take the supportive role of making it harder for people to get onto their carry.


Next, ill talk about Assassins

If mages hate chaos, assassins thrive off of it. Assassins are a lot less stable and consistent than mages. They cannot control the minion waves as well, they don't always scale into the late game the same way mages do, and they often never have tools to help support any other players. Instead, assassins thrive off of well, assassinating carries. The first goal of an assassin champion is to kill their direct laner if they can. While good if given the opportunity, its not always possible due to how safe some mages can be, so they find an alternative by using the fact that they get the value of being in the central point of the map. Due to being in the middle of the game, it unlocks access to opportunities everywhere. Assassins are very good with working with their junglers, helping fight over objectives in the early and mid stages of the game, and roaming down to the bot lane where eliminating carries is a very valuable goal. The primary goals for an assassin at the end of the day are to create chaos and kill the enemy carries in fights. Assassins are probably the most cerebral class of character, not always having clear points of when and what to be doing at a given time.


Lastly, Duelists (and sometimes bruisers)

Just like top lane, mid lane is still a 1 on 1 role. Some champions from the top lane can be pretty valuable in the mid lane too if they have great matchups verses the cast of champions that frequent the mid lane. Normally duelists that don't mind, or even thrive, off of having a shorter lane are picked here. Duelists in the mid lane fill a very similar niche to assassins in the mid lane, the key difference is that assassins normally deal damage in large bursts and are able to quickly get in and get out, where duelists normally do slower but more consistent high damage and try to stick on to their opponents rather than escaping after the fight. Bruisers in the mid lane are normally just picked as counters to strong specific champions that are picked often, but can be played very similarly to duelists.



Mid lane is probably the most complex role with the most depth to the amount of differing strategies that work in the role, which also means it has a very large range of different types of characters and playstyles. I think Mid lane is a good starting point if you are unsure what you would like to try, mid lane can be a very "jack of all trades, master of none" role a lot of the time. Mid lane has aspects of Top lane with being a one on one and needing to focus on working with minion waves, but its a lot more simple to do that with a lot of mid lane champions and a shorter lane. Mid lane has aspects of ADC by being able to be played as a carry role, sometimes even being stronger than the actual ADC in the game, and needing similar positioning skills as the ADC. Mid can be played like support if you pick supportive mages and play around your carries and play to protect rather than damage yourself. Lastly mid can be played like a jungler by focusing of roaming out of your lane and ganking enemies that overextend or just playing to help secure objectives on the map with your jungler. Mid lane builds the very core skills of the overall game of League of Legends better than any other role. I recommend mid lane to anybody that wants to learn every aspect of the game a decent amount, but not be 100% dialed in to one set strategy always.
