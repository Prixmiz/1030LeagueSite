The "Protector" of the game, Support


Support is the second role in the bottom lane. Support is unique compared to every other laner in which they do not need to go for last hits on minions! Instead, they buy cheaper items and play with a lot less gold in general than every other role, which means they tend to try to find other ways to be useful outside of damage (but not always!). Support used to be a very simple and straight forward role, and in some ways still is; protect the ADC early, and let the ADC carry you late game. The typical gameplan of a support is to make sure your ADC comes out ahead in whatever they need to do, either dueling in 2 verses 2 or making sure nobody can get to the ADC by protecting them from others. Because support doesn't need to be last hitting minions, that also opens supports up to do kinda whatever you want in your down time. You can go mid lane, top lane, help your jungler, or just keep protecting your ADC. Supports very a lot with playstyle with types of supports, so instead of going through everything you could possibly do, ill go through everything every class likes to do.


Enchanters

Enchanters are the types of characters people think when people think of a protective role; healing, shielding, and keeping people at bay with crowd control. Enchanters typically want to stay by the ADC's side for most of the game and always be protecting at all costs. If youre looking to play support to actually be as supportive as possible, enchanters are the way to go.


Tank Supports

Tank supports are inherently a bit more aggressive and in your face than enchanters. Enchanters want to keep you away, while tank supports want to get into the action and create a bit more chaos. Tank supports are normally paired well with early game ADCs to try to catch out people walking a little too close and engaging in to soak up damage while your ADC does the damage. Tanks can still defend too though, with lots of crowd control like stuns and knock-ups, they are very good at keeping people right where you want them. While normally best used as a more aggressive counterpart to an ADC, tank supports can also play the role of a roaming support, leaving the ADC to go into top, mid, or help their jungler do whatever task they plan on doing. At the end of the day, a tank supports job is to get into the chaos and take damage for the team while others can use the opportunity to dish out damage from behind them.


Mage Supports

Mages are here too! while getting less gold than playing a mage in the Mid or ADC role, mages still function pretty well as supports by supporting with damage instead of typical healing or crowd control. Mages supports look to go for a lot of poking damage on the ADC or enemy support, and love taking as many fights as possible using their higher damage than other supports. The problem with mage supports though, is you dont have gold from minions to rely on to get strong, and they're pretty weak at leaving the ADC to go roam, so they must stay in lane and play purely to fight 2 verses 2.



Support is a bit more now than just being a "supportive" player of the team. You can play support as a carry style player, or take the defensive role in protecting allies. Support champions are typically stronger than their ADC counterparts, so its the Supports job to make sure the early laning goes well for the duo. Support players need to learn good 2 verses 2 matchups, larger pools of champions to play from, and many different styles of being supportive for the team to find success. If last hitting minions isn't for you, or you want to take a more defensive role in the game by protecting others, then Support is the role for you.
