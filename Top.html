The Dueling Role, Top Lane


Top lane is one of two solo lanes in League of Legends, meaning you play in the lane alone verses the other solo lane opponent, Mid lane being the other solo lane. Top lane being the longer lane of the two means you have a lot more space for strategic decision making based off of minion waves (the resource you fight over to build leads with the gold given by last hitting). Because the lane is so large, champions that are able to fight longer - more drawn out fights tend to be very strong in Top lane. The types of champions that are most likely to be found in the top lane are:


Tanks


Champions with very high health pools and resistances, built to play it safer in early stages of the game and be utility for the team later.

Bruisers


Champions that have a bit of bulk and resistances but play to fight early in short bursts when the time is right while having tools to help start fights for themselves or sometimes their teammates.

Duelists

Champions that have more average defenses, and lower ability to help their teammates, but specialize very heavily into fighting their laner and building massive leads to become as large of a thread as possible, but being able to do very little if behind.

Marksmen top

Champions with long range and options to escape duels when the enemy champion gets into their range. Due to most Top lane champions being very short range, some specific ranged carry type champions that would normally be seen in the ADC role can be seen here as a way to counter pick champions that can not deal with the poke and run-away playstyle. Typically when duelists or bruisers do get an opportunity to catch them and get into their range, they have a harder time than others surviving fights.



Top lane is a role that is very focused on the 1 verses 1 aspect of the game. While other teammates sometimes do come to help in the top lane at times, Top lane is the role that has the least interaction with the less of the team from both sides, typically preferring the duel with their direct opponent rather than focusing on playing with and around their teammates. Because of this, top laners get a lot of value from deep understanding of your champion's matchups, when and how to pick champions that counter your opponents champion, and how to utilize minion waves to fight in positions you want for yourself and your counterpart does not want to fight in. Top lane will build very deep understanding of the core minion wave and fighting systems of the game. Top lane if for you if you're a person that wants to defer from focusing on team gameplay and would rather win based off of your ability to master a characters fighting strengths and playing purely to win a 1 on 1 duel till the end.
