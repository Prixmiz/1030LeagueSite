What role is right for me in League of Legends?


League of Legends is a 5 verses 5 team based strategy game that has 5 unique roles mirroring each other on each team. Each role in League has its own unique take on how to contribute to your team, skills to build to improve, and even just different types of character archetypes with in game playstyle and out of game character themes. This website will be a starting point on what you can expect from each position, what skills will be built, what kind of characters are found in each role, and even what kind of people gravitate towards each playstyle.
